## One-line description

> écentic is the merchant-side optimization layer for agent commerce.

## Five-line description

écentic helps online stores get picked when AI shopping agents do the buying. The platform simulates how ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity compare a merchant's products against competitors, diagnoses why they win or lose each comparison, and auto-publishes optimised listings back to the merchant's store. The company is live on the Shopify App Store with paying merchants, and is engaged in active enterprise pilot conversations through Plug and Play Tirana's corporate ventures network. Founded by Xhurian Shaba (CEO), Alvi Palloshi (CTO), and Iseld Muça (Head of Sales). Headquartered in Tirana, Albania.

## The category

écentic operates in the **agent commerce optimization** category — a category that emerged in 2025–2026 alongside the launch of AI agent commerce protocols (Google Universal Commerce Protocol, OpenAI Agentic Commerce Protocol, Visa Trusted Agent Protocol, Mastercard Agent Pay). The category sits at the intersection of e-commerce SaaS and AI infrastructure.

It is distinct from **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**, which optimises for being mentioned in chatbot answers. écentic optimises for being chosen as the cart — the merchant that AI agents actually buy from.

## What makes écentic different

Three claims, each defensible:

1. **Active simulation.** écentic stages real competitive buying sessions across GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, telling merchants their actual agent win rate — not an estimate or sentiment score.
2. **Closed-loop automation.** écentic doesn't suggest fixes. It writes the fix (title, description, structured data, UCP profile) and publishes it back to the merchant's store automatically.
3. **First on UCP + ACP.** écentic is the first tool that scores readiness across both Google's Universal Commerce Protocol and OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol.

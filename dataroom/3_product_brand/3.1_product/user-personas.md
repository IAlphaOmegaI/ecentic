# écentic — User Personas

**Last updated:** 2026-06-02

These are the four merchant-side personas écentic is built for. They are not consumer personas — those are modelled separately inside the simulation engine itself (budget shopper, premium shopper, eco-conscious shopper). The personas below are the people who buy, install, and use écentic.

---

## Persona 1: The Indie Founder

**Name (composite):** Anya, 31
**Role:** Founder and head of everything at a 7-person DTC brand on Shopify
**GMV:** €800K / year
**Geography:** Berlin (markets across DACH and UK)

### Job to be done
"Make sure my brand shows up when people ask AI for recommendations in my category — without me having to learn a whole new optimisation discipline."

### Day-in-the-life pain
- Sees GPTBot, ClaudeBot in her traffic logs and doesn't know what to do about it
- Spends Saturdays in front of ChatGPT testing whether her brand shows up; sometimes it does, sometimes a competitor does, and she can't predict which
- Knows that AI shopping is "the next thing" but can't justify a full-time SEO/AI hire
- Reads Twitter and Lenny's Newsletter; treats tooling decisions as 24-hour decisions

### What she needs from écentic
- One-click install (no developer)
- Free scan that gives her one concrete reason her hero product loses to her main competitor
- Pricing under €150/mo (so she can sign up without asking anyone)
- Honest, plain-English explanation of *why* she's losing — not jargon, not GEO marketing

### How she discovers us
- Shopify App Store organic search ("AI optimisation")
- YouTube tutorial from a creator she trusts
- A Twitter/X thread about Cyber Week 2025 agent traffic
- The free public UCP validator (she enters her domain, gets a report, then installs)

### How she pays
- Credit card on Shopify billing, monthly
- Upgrade decisions happen in-product, not in a sales conversation
- Will churn if she logs in for 30 days and doesn't see a clear "we shipped X fixes" report

### Tier fit
Connect ($149/mo) initially, upgrades to Compete ($499/mo) within 60 days if she likes the auto-publish loop.

## Persona 2: The Growth Lead at a DTC Brand

**Name (composite):** Marcus, 34
**Role:** Head of Growth at a $5M GMV beauty brand on Shopify Plus
**GMV:** €4M / year
**Geography:** New York (sells to US + UK)

### Job to be done
"Find the next 5% lift in revenue. AI shopping is a channel that didn't exist last year — I want to be early."

### Day-in-the-life pain
- Has reduced returns on Meta ads, looking for new channels
- Already invested in conversion rate optimisation, email, retention — running out of easy wins
- Reports to a founder who wants to know "what's next" every two weeks
- Saw a competitor get recommended by ChatGPT and immediately Slacked the team about it

### What he needs from écentic
- Numbers, not vibes. Specific win-rate deltas per SKU per model.
- A weekly delta report he can forward to his founder ("Here's what we shipped this week, here's what moved.")
- Auto-publish that he can review and approve, not unsupervised changes
- A path to API access so he can pull data into his internal BI

### How he discovers us
- Word-of-mouth from a peer in another DTC brand
- Newsletter / podcast mention (Modern Retail, Lenny's, DTC Newsletter)
- Outbound from écentic's GTM Lead with a personalised scan report attached

### How he pays
- Annual contract at 15% discount on Compete or Dominate
- Procurement involvement is light — he can sign up to ~€10K/year on his own authority
- Renewal decision sits with him; he treats it like any other tooling renewal

### Tier fit
Compete ($499/mo) for the first 3–6 months, upgrades to Dominate ($999/mo) when he wants persona-based simulation and the API. May commission a one-off catalog audit before a major Q4 launch.

## Persona 3: The Agency Operator

**Name (composite):** Olivia, 38
**Role:** Director of Performance at a Shopify Plus partner agency
**Manages:** 12 merchant clients, €5M–€40M GMV each
**Geography:** London

### Job to be done
"Bring my clients a credible answer to 'what are you doing about AI shopping' that I can deliver as a managed service."

### Day-in-the-life pain
- Clients are starting to ask about AI agents and she has no good answer
- Competing against agencies that are pitching "agentic commerce" as a service line — she needs the tooling
- Doesn't want to build a tool — wants to white-label or resell something that exists
- Margins are thin; she needs a 20%+ resale margin to justify selling it

### What she needs from écentic
- A way to manage multiple merchant accounts from one login
- Shareable scan reports she can rebrand for client meetings
- Volume discount or agency tier pricing
- A clear story about how écentic differs from GEO tools (which her competitors are pitching)

### How she discovers us
- Shopify Plus partner directory and partner Slack
- Outbound from écentic's agency partner outreach
- A client of hers (Marcus, above) recommends she try it

### How she pays
- Agency-managed contract — typically annual, €15K–€30K total covering multiple client accounts
- Co-marketing arrangement: she promotes écentic to her client roster, gets 20% resale margin
- Wants joint case studies for her own marketing

### Tier fit
Compete ($499/mo) × multiple client accounts, with an agency-tier discount. Multi-account billing handled at the agency level.

## Persona 4: The Enterprise Operator

**Name (composite):** Karim, 45
**Role:** VP, Digital Commerce at a Tier-1 retail brand (think Carrefour, eBay, Inditex)
**GMV:** €50M+ / year for his line of business
**Geography:** Paris, with EU and US scope

### Job to be done
"Get my organisation a defensible, board-presentable view of where we stand on agent-mediated commerce — before our CEO asks."

### Day-in-the-life pain
- Has been asked by the C-suite about agent commerce; has no operational answer
- Internal teams are exploring multiple AI initiatives in silos
- Procurement and security teams will scrutinise any vendor heavily
- Needs to prove ROI before he can scale spend

### What he needs from écentic
- A pilot that is structured (clear KPI, clear timeline, clear go/no-go gate)
- A vendor that is not a single-person shop, with a credible compliance posture (SOC 2 in progress, EU data residency live, GDPR DPA ready)
- Co-published, mutually approved case study he can show internally
- Aggregate industry benchmarks to position his organisation
- A path to a meaningful annual contract after the pilot

### How he discovers us
- Plug and Play corporate ventures introduction
- Payment-network partnership (Visa Trusted Agent Protocol angle for Carrefour specifically)
- Industry-conference panels and Visa Merchant Observatory positioning
- Direct outbound from écentic's CEO

### How he pays
- 12-week pilot, free for the first cohort (2026); €15K–€50K from 2027 onwards
- Annual contract at €25K–€75K Year 1
- Procurement, legal, and IT security all sign off
- Renewal decision goes through a vendor review committee

### Tier fit
Enterprise, custom-priced. Multi-stakeholder sale cycle of 3–6 months.

## How these personas drive product decisions

- **Anya** drives the App Store onboarding flow, the free scan as lead magnet, and the pricing of Connect tier.
- **Marcus** drives the auto-publish loop, the weekly delta report, and the API access on Dominate tier.
- **Olivia** drives the multi-account management features, the shareable scan reports, and the agency partner programme.
- **Karim** drives the 12-week pilot framework, the security and compliance posture, and the case-study generation programme.

When we say "no" to a feature, it is typically because none of the four personas above clearly benefit. When we say "yes," it is typically because at least two of them benefit and at least one of them would churn without it.

# écentic — Team Bios

**Last updated:** 2026-06-02

The écentic team is three shareholding founders and two long-tenured contractors. All work full-time on the company. Headquarters: Tirana, Albania.

---

## Founders & Shareholders

### Xhurian Shaba — Founder, Chief Executive Officer
**Equity:** 40,000 Common Shares (40%)
**Role:** Sets product strategy, owns engineering direction, leads investor and partner conversations.

Xhurian is a serial founder with three companies under his belt before écentic. He has been shipping software into the Shopify ecosystem since 2023, where he learned first-hand how the catalog, payments, and checkout layers actually compose. Most recently he led development at a Canadian startup that reached a meaningful operating scale, where his work focused on building production-grade commerce infrastructure under live merchant traffic. The lessons from that scale — what survives Black Friday and what doesn't — sit underneath the écentic codebase today.

At écentic, Xhurian owns the engineering organization, makes the architecture decisions, and pulls weight directly in the codebase every week. He is also the primary investor-facing voice.

**Location:** Tirana, Albania.
**Reachable:** xhurian.shaba@ecentic.ai

---

### Alvi Palloshi — Founder, Chief Technology Officer
**Equity:** 30,000 Common Shares (30%)
**Role:** Owns infrastructure, security, and core data platform. Manages the production environment.

Alvi has held CTO and senior engineering positions across several UK and Europe-based startups, where his work spanned data infrastructure, application security, and team building from zero. His career has been a sustained stress-test of what it takes to run a small team that ships reliably without an enterprise budget.

At écentic, Alvi is responsible for the platform spine: the PostgreSQL data model, the Drizzle ORM layer, the job queue that runs simulations at scale, and the security posture that has to satisfy enterprise procurement teams from day one. He drives the SOC 2 preparation work and the EU data residency design.

**Location:** Elbasan, Albania.
**Reachable:** alvi.palloshi@ecentic.ai

## Why this team wins

Three founders, three companies built together before écentic. The team has shipped, broken, and recovered together — not learning to work with each other on a fund's money. The functional split is clean: Xhurian on engineering and strategy, Alvi on infrastructure and security, Iseld on sales and partnerships. Mario and Arbi extend the coverage into product/brand and AI engineering without diluting the founder cap table.

The combined Shopify ecosystem time is approximately three years. The combined production AI deployment time is several years. Nobody on this team is learning ecommerce or AI engineering for the first time.

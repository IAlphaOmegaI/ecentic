## Burn impact

The full Year 1 cumulative cost of the hiring plan is approximately **€95K** (staggered start dates), against a total Year 1 cost run-rate captured in the Financial Model. This leaves the remainder of the €300K raise to fund founder salaries (which start at M3), infrastructure scaling, marketing investment, and a six-month buffer at exit of Year 1 if the SaaS ramp underperforms.

## Compensation philosophy

- **Founder salaries:** €1,500 / month each starting M3. Below-market, reflective of capital constraints. Reviewed at the Year 1 closing of next round.
- **Engineering salaries:** €2,500 gross / month. At or slightly above Tirana market for mid-senior engineers; below Western European market by design.
- **GTM and marketing:** €2,800 gross / month. Slightly above engineering to reflect direct revenue accountability.
- **Equity for new hires:** All hires receive option grants from the 10% ESOP pool, vesting on the standard 4-year / 1-year cliff schedule per the Stock Option Plan. Senior hires (Lead-level) receive 0.5%–1.5% on a fully diluted basis. Engineer-level hires receive 0.25%–0.75%.

## Hires NOT planned in Year 1

These are intentional decisions, not oversights:

- **No COO / VP-level hires.** Founders run their respective functions through Year 1; senior leadership decisions wait until product-market-fit is harder to challenge.
- **No designer.** Mario covers product and brand design as a contractor; we will revisit once we have >€500K ARR.
- **No dedicated SRE / DevOps.** Alvi (CTO) and Engineer #1 cover infrastructure; the codebase is hostable on Vercel + managed PostgreSQL until scale warrants otherwise.
- **No US-based salesperson.** Iseld covers US and EU enterprise from Tirana via remote selling; physical US presence postponed to Series A.

## Hiring sequence triggers (when to pull hires forward or push back)

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| App Store install rate < 50 / week by M3 | Pull GTM/Sales Lead forward to M2 |
| Enterprise pilot signed before M6 | Pull Customer Success forward to M9 |
| WooCommerce integration delayed | Pull Engineer #2 forward to M5 |
| MRR > €25K by M6 | Add a second GTM hire (account executive) at M8 |
| MRR < €10K by M9 | Freeze marketing and CS hires; reallocate to engineering |

## Geographic distribution

All hires expected to be Tirana-based or remote-EU through Year 1. This keeps salaries efficient, simplifies legal and tax structuring (Albanian SHPK employment contracts), and preserves the company's existing cultural cohesion. Remote-EU hiring opens up once we have ≥ 8 people and the operational muscle to support distributed work.

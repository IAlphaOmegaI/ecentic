## Notes on running this monthly

- **Cadence is non-negotiable.** Skipping a month signals trouble. If a month is bad, send a short email anyway.
- **Length cap: one screen on a laptop.** If the update doesn't fit, cut content, don't break the format.
- **No marketing copy.** This is the channel where investors come to find out what is actually happening. Don't undermine it with promotional language.
- **Numbers must reconcile across artifacts.** The MRR in this email must match the MRR in the Key Metrics Dashboard and the pitch deck. If there's any drift, fix it before sending.
- **Archive every update.** Each month's update gets saved to the data room at `dataroom/1_company_fundamentals/1.4_investment_docs/updates/[YYYY-MM]-investor-update.md`.

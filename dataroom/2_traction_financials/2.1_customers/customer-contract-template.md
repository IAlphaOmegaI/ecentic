## 4. Fees and Payment

### 4.1 Fees
Customer shall pay the fees set out in the Order Form or in the in-product checkout, in the currency specified.

### 4.2 Invoicing
Unless otherwise stated, fees are invoiced monthly in advance for monthly plans and annually in advance for annual plans. Annual plans receive the discount stated in the applicable pricing page or Order Form.

### 4.3 Payment terms
Invoices are due net thirty (30) days for invoiced customers; credit-card billed customers are charged on the renewal date. Late payments accrue interest at the lesser of (a) the European Central Bank main refinancing rate plus five percent (5%) per annum, and (b) the maximum permitted by applicable law.

### 4.4 Taxes
Fees are exclusive of any value-added tax, sales tax, or other taxes that may apply. Customer is responsible for all such taxes, except taxes based on écentic's net income.

écentic may suspend the Service for non-payment after thirty (30) days' written notice. Customer Data will be retained during suspension and for a further ninety (90) days following termination, subject to Section 9.

## 5. Term and Termination

### 5.1 Term
The initial term is set out in the Order Form. Monthly subscriptions auto-renew month-to-month until cancelled. Annual subscriptions auto-renew for additional twelve-month periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

### 5.2 Termination for cause
Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of written notice of the breach.

### 5.3 Termination for convenience
Customer may cancel monthly subscriptions at any time effective end-of-month. Annual subscriptions may not be terminated for convenience during the paid period; pro-rated refunds are not available except where required by applicable consumer law.

### 5.4 Effect of termination
Upon termination: (a) Customer's right to access the Service ceases; (b) écentic will retain Customer Data for ninety (90) days, during which Customer may request export in a structured machine-readable format; (c) after ninety (90) days écentic will delete Customer Data, except for backups retained per écentic's standard backup-retention policy and any data legally required to be retained.

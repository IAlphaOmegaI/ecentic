## What AI visibility tracking measures

Before the list, a short note on terms, because vendors use them loosely.

A **mention** is your brand name appearing in an AI answer. A **citation** is the AI linking to your page as the source. They are not the same thing, and a store can collect plenty of mentions while a competitor takes every citation in its category.

The metrics that matter for a store:

- **Visibility share**: how often your brand appears across a fixed prompt set.
- **Share of voice**: your presence against named competitors on the same prompts.
- **Citation share**: which pages AI links to when it answers.
- **Sentiment**: how the answer describes you.
- **Win rate**: how often your product is the one recommended, prompt by prompt.

A Perplexity "rank" is not a rank in the Google sense. It is a position in one generated answer, and it can change between sessions. Track the pattern across a fixed prompt set, not a single screenshot.

## A 30-day playbook for a Shopify or WooCommerce store

Here is how to use a tracker without wasting the first month on dashboards.

1. **Pick 20 to 50 SKUs from two or three categories.** Choose products where you already rank on page one of organic search but suspect a competitor is taking the AI citation. That gap is easier to close than a category you have never won.
2. **Write your prompt set from real buyer questions.** Use the ones your support inbox gets. "Best running shoes for flat feet under $120" and "best moisturizer for dry skin under $40" are the shape. Keep the set fixed so the numbers compare week to week.
3. **Record the baseline.** Note visibility share, citation share and win rate per prompt before you change anything. If you want to see how different tools present ChatGPT answers, the guide on how to [rank in ChatGPT](https://ecentic.ai/blog/rank-in-chatgpt) walks through what each one shows.
4. **Read the citation sources.** When a competitor wins, look at which page AI cited. Often it is a category page or a comparison article, not a product page.
5. **Fix the product data first.** A citation report does not fix the [product feed for AI](https://ecentic.ai/blog/product-feed-for-ai) that the assistant reads. Missing attributes, thin descriptions and broken schema are the usual causes, and they are cheap to fix.
6. **Publish the change and tag it.** Date the edit so you can tie it to a later shift in citations.
7. **Check attribution.** AI referrals often land as direct traffic in analytics, so set up [AI attribution for ecommerce](https://ecentic.ai/blog/ai-attribution-ecommerce) before you judge results.
8. **Re-run the same prompts after two to four weeks.** Compare against the baseline. If nothing moved, the fix was probably not the constraint.

If you are choosing between tools, the guide to the [best AI visibility tools for Shopify stores](https://ecentic.ai/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools-for-shopify) covers the tracker side in more detail.

When the report shows your products missing, the next step is to see what the assistant reads on the page. Running [agent simulations](https://ecentic.ai/platform/simulate) against the live catalog shows which attribute decided the answer, so the fix goes to the right field.

## FAQ

### What is an AEO tool?
An Answer Engine Optimization tool tracks how AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews describe and cite your brand, then helps you improve it. Some only report. Others, like Cognizo, also publish the content and measure what it did.

### Do I need a tracker if I already have an SEO tool?
If your SEO suite has added AI visibility data, you have a starting point. The question is whether it can show you per-page crawls, clicks and conversions from AI, and whether it can publish a fix. If not, you will still be exporting a report and briefing a writer.

### How long before AI visibility changes?
Plan on two to four weeks before a re-run of the same prompt set shows movement, and longer for competitive categories. Keep the prompt set fixed and change one thing at a time so you can tell what worked.

### Can an agency resell these tools under its own brand?
Some can. Cognizo runs the full platform on the agency's own domain with its own name, logo and email accounts, and quotes white-label pricing by number of client brands. Others offer agency plans, with the level of branding varying by vendor.

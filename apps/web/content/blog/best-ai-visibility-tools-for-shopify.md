The best AI visibility tool for a Shopify store depends on whether you want to watch what ChatGPT and Perplexity say about you or change the product data they read. For a mid-size DTC brand on Shopify Plus, Profound, Scrunch and Peec AI are the strongest trackers, while Swap Discovery (formerly Vizby) and ecentic write product fixes back into Shopify. On a small budget, start with StoreSEO's free plan for the on-page basics and Otterly at $29 a month for tracking.

Shopify already handles the listing. Its agentic storefronts, on by default for eligible stores, put your products in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Meta's AI surfaces [1]. Getting picked over the other products in the answer is where these tools earn their monthly fee, or don't.

Fair caveat: I run ecentic, and it's on this list. Every competitor fact here comes from the vendor's own pages, checked on 23 or 27 October 2026.

**Last reviewed: 27 October 2026.** Prices are monthly, in US dollars.

## How I sorted the tools

Every tool here says it gets Shopify products into AI answers, in one of three ways. I think picking the wrong way costs more than picking the wrong vendor.

- **Trackers** run your buyers' questions on the assistants and report how often your brand or products come up.
- **On-page apps** fix the markup and files AI crawlers read, such as structured data and llms.txt.
- **Product fixers** work out why an assistant picked a competitor's product and change your product data.

The table compares Shopify setup, store changes and price. The catalog side has its own post: [GEO tools for ecommerce product catalogs](/blog/geo-tools-for-ecommerce-product-catalogs).

## Trackers: watching what the assistants say

### Profound

Profound is the enterprise pick. A trial runs 50 prompts a day for 7 days on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, then it's a custom Enterprise contract with up to nine answer engines and ChatGPT Shopping tracking [2]. I think its reporting is the deepest here. It lists no Shopify integration, so your team makes the changes.

### Scrunch

Core is $250 a month for 125 prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Copilot, with site audits, AI bot traffic and a 7-day free trial. Enterprise covers nine assistants and adds product observability and AXP, its platform for serving pages built for AI agents [3]. The Shopping view shows which of your products come up in AI shopping answers next to competitors', and which retailers get the click [4]. I couldn't find a Shopify integration on its pricing page.

### Peec AI

Peec added AI Shopping Analytics in June 2026. It reads your Shopify store's public product feed and gives each product a row with visibility, win rate, position and the price the assistant quotes [5]. Starter is $95 a month for 50 prompts on three models, with Perplexity as a paid add-on [6]. More in [Peec AI alternatives](/blog/peec-ai-alternatives).

### AthenaHQ

AthenaHQ has a free Essential plan with 300 credits (one credit is one AI response) and Starter at $295 a month for 3,600 credits across 11 models, with a Shopify integration in the plan comparison [7]. Its e-commerce page says it tracks visibility per product and writes product descriptions for AI [8]. I couldn't find whether those descriptions go into Shopify for you, so ask on the demo.

### Anagram

Anagram pairs a visibility report, showing how your brand appears in ChatGPT next to competitors [9], with a Site Agent: a chat assistant on your own store. Its Shopify connection syncs your catalog daily for the chat, and the docs don't describe writing changes back [10]. Site Agent starts at $0 a month with 100 engagements, and paid tiers at $299 [11]. For the visibility report's price, check their site.

### Otterly

Otterly is the cheapest way to watch. Lite is $29 a month for 15 prompts, checked daily on ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot, with Gemini and Claude as paid add-ons [12]. It works at the brand level. I compared it with ours in [ecentic vs Otterly](/blog/ecentic-vs-otterly).

## StoreSEO: the on-page basics

StoreSEO is a Shopify SEO app by Storeware, rated 5.0 from 772 reviews. The free plan covers 25 products and includes an llms.txt generator (a plain file telling AI crawlers what your store offers); Lite is $14.99 a month. It also adds JSON-LD, the structured data agents read on a product page, plus FAQ schema, and fixes meta tags and alt text in bulk [13]. It reports orders from AI answer engines [14], but doesn't say it checks whether your products come up in answers. I think it's a sensible first install for a small store: it fixes the plumbing every other tool assumes is right.

## Product fixers: changing what the assistants read

### Swap Discovery (formerly Vizby)

Vizby now sells as Swap Discovery, still listed on the Shopify App Store by Vizby AI Ltd. It runs buyer prompts, builds an action plan (product JSON-LD, product and collection rewrites, FAQs), and you approve each change or let it apply them [15]. Business is $79 a month on ChatGPT and Gemini, with a 7-day trial; Perplexity comes with Growth at $249. Business optimizes 20 products a month [16], and it's Shopify only. More in [Vizby alternatives](/blog/vizby-alternatives).

### ecentic

ecentic helps online stores sell when AI agents do the shopping. We have ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini compare your products with the competitors you name and pick one, show you why a product gets skipped, and write the fix to the title, description and structured data. On pay-as-you-go, Scale and Dominate the fix goes to Shopify when you click Apply; Compete gives field-by-field suggestions. We also track the visits AI assistants send your Shopify store, week over week.

Pay-as-you-go costs $0 until an agent drives a sale, then 3% of orders that follow a visit from an AI assistant in the previous 7 days, capped at $500 a month, with everything in Dominate. Compete is $14.90 a month for up to 100 products, Scale $44.90 for up to 1,000, and Dominate $149, which adds Google Merchant Center sync and checks the Merchant Center settings Google's agent checkout needs [17]. The Win Rate Report is on every plan.

The trade-offs: we don't check Perplexity, and we're newer, with one published pilot result. DUA Flavours (pilot, published with permission): visits from AI assistants and direct traffic rose 12% between April and July 2026 [18]. Visits, not sales.

## What's the best AI search optimization tool for a mid-size DTC brand on Shopify Plus?

I'd pick by who will act on it. If a brand or SEO team reports AI search to a CMO, start with a tracker: Profound for an enterprise contract, Scrunch to start on a published plan, Peec AI for per-product tracking from $95 a month.

If your e-commerce team owns the product pages and is short on hours, a tracker just lengthens their to-do list. Swap Discovery suits a Shopify-only brand selling a few dozen hero products. ecentic suits a catalog of hundreds of products, a brand that also runs WooCommerce, or one that wants the bill tied to AI-attributed orders. Past 1,000 products or with several expansion stores, you'd be on our Enterprise plan, with custom limits and every store in one account [17].

Plenty of brands run one of each. Two trackers mostly deliver the same bad news in two dashboards.

## Which tools get Shopify products recommended by ChatGPT and Perplexity?

No tool can make an assistant recommend you. They show where you lose, and the fixers change the reason.

For ChatGPT, Shopify's agentic storefronts handle the listing for eligible stores, and shoppers finish on your own store's checkout [1]. For Perplexity, which Shopify doesn't list, you need a tracker that covers it: Otterly Lite, Scrunch Core and Swap Discovery's Growth plan include it, and Peec sells it as an add-on. ecentic doesn't check Perplexity.

Then the answer names a few products and picks one. What decides it is product data the assistant can read: a title that says what the thing is, the size or material the buyer asked about, and a price and stock level that match across your page, feed and Merchant Center.

## FAQ

### Do I need one of these tools if Shopify already lists my products in ChatGPT?

Shopify gets your products into the channel but doesn't tell you how often the assistant picks them, or why it didn't. With a handful of products and little competition, asking ChatGPT your buyer's question once a month may be enough.

### Do any of these tools price differently for Shopify Plus?

Not that I found. They price by prompts, credits, engagements or products, so what Plus changes is scale. Check the limit against your catalog, and ask how expansion stores count.

### Will an llms.txt file get my products recommended?

It tells AI crawlers what your store offers. I haven't seen evidence that the file alone changes which product an assistant picks, so treat it as cheap housekeeping.

### Can I run a tracker and a product fixer together?

Yes, as long as one tool owns titles, descriptions and structured data. Two apps rewriting the same fields undo each other's work.

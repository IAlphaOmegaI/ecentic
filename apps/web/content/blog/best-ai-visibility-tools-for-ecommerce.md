AI visibility tools for an online store come in two kinds. Measuring tools such as Otterly, Peec AI, Profound, Semrush, Ahrefs Brand Radar and Scrunch count how often ChatGPT, Gemini and other assistants mention your brand or products. Changing tools such as Swap Discovery (formerly Vizby) and ecentic rewrite the product data those assistants read, so pick by what you're missing: the number or the fix.

Every SEO suite grew an AI visibility tab this year. Ahrefs now maps 509 million prompts people ask AI platforms [1], and somewhere in there is the one your buyer typed. I think a store owner needs a smaller number first: which of my products lost, to whom, and which field cost the sale.

Fair caveat: I run ecentic, which sits in the second group. Everything about the other tools comes from their own pricing and product pages, and I tried to say who each one fits.

**Last reviewed: 27 October 2026.** Prices are monthly, in US dollars, from each vendor's own page, checked between 23 and 27 October 2026.

## Measuring and changing are two different jobs

A measuring tool runs your buyers' questions on a schedule and records whether you're named, in what position and which sites the answer cites. Someone on your team then does the editing. A changing tool starts from your catalog, works out what the assistant needed and didn't find, and writes to the title, description, structured data or feed.

In a store, assistants compare products on price, stock, sizes and shipping, and on Google AI Mode and Gemini in the US the shopper can buy inside the answer through UCP, Google's standard for agent checkout [2]. A mention gets you into the answer. The product data decides who gets the order.

## Tools that measure how often AI mentions you

**Otterly** is the cheapest way in. Lite is $29 a month for 15 prompts, checked daily on ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot, with Gemini as a $9 add-on [3]. It covers 65 countries and languages and audits your pages the way AI crawlers see them [4], but it doesn't import a catalog.

**Peec AI** is the strongest pure measurement option for a catalog. Starter is $95 a month for 50 prompts on three models you choose [5]. Its AI Shopping Analytics reads your Shopify storefront, a CSV or a Merchant Center feed and gives each product its own row, with visibility, position and the price the assistant quotes [6].

**Profound** is built for large brands. A free trial runs 50 prompts a day for 7 days on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. After that it's a custom Enterprise contract, which is where ChatGPT Shopping tracking sits [7].

**Semrush** sells AI Visibility from $99 a month per domain, billed annually, for 25 custom prompts on ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini and Perplexity [8].

**Ahrefs Brand Radar** tracks mentions, citations and AI share of voice on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Overviews and AI Mode, with Claude on its AI Visibility Index. Custom prompts start at $50 a month and the Index at $199 [1].

**Scrunch** covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and Meta on every plan. Starter is $300 a month, or $250 billed annually, for 350 custom prompts [9]. It also lists AXP, which generates an AI-friendly version of your site.

**AthenaHQ** sits between the groups. Starter is $295 a month for 3,600 credits across 11 models, with a free Essential plan [10]. Its e-commerce page says it tracks visibility per product and writes product descriptions for AI [11]. I couldn't find whether those go into your store, so ask on the demo.

## Best platforms for tracking how often ChatGPT and Gemini mention my products

The question is about products, and most trackers count the brand. For single products in real answers, Peec AI is the most direct fit: put ChatGPT and Gemini among your three Starter models, import the catalog, and each product gets its own row [5][6]. AthenaHQ says it tracks per product too, and Profound tracks ChatGPT Shopping on Enterprise.

Otterly, Semrush, Ahrefs and Scrunch can follow a product if you write a prompt for it. That works for ten hero products. For a few hundred it gets expensive, because every product costs prompts and every prompt runs on each engine you track.

ecentic doesn't count mentions in live answers. It asks which product the assistant picks when it compares yours with a named competitor's, and why. I think that's the number closest to a sale, since an answer names a few products and the shopper clicks one of them.

## Otterly alternatives for ecommerce product optimization

Otterly does a lot well: a low entry price, wide engine coverage and page audits. Where it stops for a store is the catalog. It doesn't import your products, and its fixes are recommendations your team makes by hand [4].

So the alternative depends on why you're leaving. For per-product numbers from real answers, Peec AI. For Claude and Meta on the entry plan, Scrunch. For edits applied to the store, Swap Discovery on Shopify, or ecentic on Shopify and WooCommerce. ## FAQ

### How many prompts should a store track?

Start with one or two for each product category you sell, written the way a buyer asks, with a use and a budget in them. Twenty good prompts tell you more than two hundred variations of "best running shoes". Add prompts for a single product only when it's worth fixing.

### Is there a free way to check whether ChatGPT and Gemini mention my products?

Ask them the question your buyer would ask, then ask again a few days later, because answers vary. For more than a smoke test, Profound and Scrunch have 7-day trials, AthenaHQ has a free Essential plan, and ecentic's free Scan compares your top 10 products with competitors once.

### Does ecentic track brand mentions or citations?

No. ecentic has ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini compare your products with named competitors' products, shows why a product loses, and writes the fix. If you need mention counts, sentiment or citation sources, pair it with one of the measuring tools above.

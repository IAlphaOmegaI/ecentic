## Dashboard design follow-up

The accepted 24 November design uses one **full-viewport 3px charcoal frame**, matching the header/sidebar, around the hub, Settings and per-store screens. There is **no white outer gutter or outward shadow**. This supersedes the initial inset-frame preview. [Dashboard layout](../../apps/web/src/app/[locale]/(main)/dashboard/layout.tsx) owns the shared frame. The white inner workspace has four rounded 8px corners and a soft inward shadow; its connected data sections retain their square, one-pixel seams. The shadow stays on the stationary workspace wrapper while content scrolls inside it, and its overlay does not intercept pointer events.

Settings now has four sidebar sections: **Profile**, **Sign-in & security**, **Connected stores**, and **Plan & billing**. The account workspace also serves the hub with All stores, Reports and Settings navigation. Settings section changes close the mobile Sheet and reset the inner content scroll; a `?checkout=success` return selects Plan & billing. Store workspaces retain their six destinations, with Settings fixed as the final sidebar footer below the independently scrolling navigation. The compact account-name menu on store, Settings and hub pages ends with a separated **Sign Out** action. Sign-out preserves the current language.

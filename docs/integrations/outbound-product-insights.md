# Automatic product insights

Automatic campaign research uses the configured Azure chat deployment. A company must pass its saved campaign criteria and have an established own-product brand before contact work begins. It must also have one specific product buying detail, such as a refill mechanism, material, ingredient, care requirement or compatibility constraint.

The assessment saves `productInsight` with the feature, feature category, exact product source URL, exact source quote and internal relevance reasoning. The quote must occur in the named product page receipt; its own ingredients, specifications, care information and FAQ are eligible even when omitted from a short structured description. About pages, company-wide claims, customer reviews and recommendations for other products cannot supply the angle. The short feature must be a contiguous part of the quote. A product name, price, stock state, generic praise, medical promise or fabricated differentiator cannot supply the angle. Weak or ambiguous evidence holds the company rather than falling back to “your product page lists X.”

The evidence is a positive observation of raw product HTML. It does not assert that data is absent, that a rendered page was reviewed, that an AI shopping query ran, or that a particular recommendation, ranking or revenue result occurred. Relevance is the model's internal reasoning about a possible buying decision, not a fact that may be copied into an email.

`{productFeature}` is available in message templates and in the three editable positive-product finding fields. Factory wording attributes the feature to the product page, frames a shopper question using `{category}` and `{productFeature}`, and proposes checking whether that detail carries into recommendations. It preserves the configured founder introduction, call to action, opt-out and signature. Existing saved Playbook wording is not silently replaced when the app changes; a deliberate Playbook save applies revised wording to future drafts.

The category must be a short singular product noun that fits the saved shopper-question template, such as “cream blush stick.” A broad industry label such as “beauty and personal care” is not usable in that sentence. When the source does not support a narrower noun, the exact observed product name is the fallback. Correcting an existing unsent prospect requires an audited transaction bound to its original product receipt and current revision; it does not rewrite the original assessment.

The original product snapshot and its receipt format stay immutable. Automatic follow-up renewal compares the same product URL, name, brand, description, price and currency, and also requires the insight quote to remain in the fresh page receipt. A changed or missing quote holds the sequence; renewal never rewrites previously sent claims.

## Azure wire contract

The SDK must use `createAzure(...).chat(deployment)` for this configured deployment. The model-output schema uses required plain strings and nullable evidence objects; strict local Zod validation and exact-source checks run after generation. Azure rejects the `uri` JSON Schema format emitted by `z.url()`, so it must not appear in the provider's structured-output schema. Local saved-data validation still requires valid URLs.

The 16 November 2026 diagnostic reserved an `azureDiagnostic` integration job before each explicit request. The first request received a definitive HTTP 400 for the unsupported URI format. One corrected, separately journaled request returned HTTP 200 with valid structured output (232 input and 47 output tokens). No uncertain company research was replayed. The diagnostic command requires an explicit unused UUID and refuses to repeat an existing ID; all model requests use `maxRetries: 0`.

Contact selection matches the actual source title against both saved role lists, recognizing common full-title/acronym equivalents, punctuation and scope qualifiers. It preserves the original title in the receipt. Assistant, junior and trainee roles are excluded; senior e-commerce, digital and growth roles rank ahead of eligible general marketing managers. A caller can exclude already attempted provider person IDs, allowing the durable coordinator to choose the next eligible retained record without repeating contact enrichment or inventing identity facts.

Apollo's live email-verifier result dates the request in `webhook_result.request_initiated` and `request_completed`, rather than always dating each record with `verified_at`. The parser accepts the unchanged completion timestamp only for the exact requested string ID, a successful `email_verifier` action, and finite ordered provider dates no later than the current time. It records that timestamp's provenance separately. It never substitutes our request time, a webhook dispatch time or a cached profile email status; missing or unrelated provider dates remain unknown. Exact accepted-record and email correlation remains required before applying verification.

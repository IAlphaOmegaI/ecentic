## Policy drafting sources and implementation check

The policy rewrite was checked against the official [GDPR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679) for transparency, legal bases, rights and transfers; [California Attorney General CCPA guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) for conditional state rights; [Delaware’s Personal Data Privacy Act](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c012d/index.html) for applicable rights/appeals; and the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy). These sources do not by themselves determine the company’s statutory coverage. The [Google Consent Mode documentation](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/concepts/consent-mode) established that loading GA with denied storage can still send cookieless pings; implementation was therefore changed to defer SDK loading until consent, rather than retaining the prior inaccurate claim.

Privacy now distinguishes account/controller processing from merchant/processor processing, lists sources and purposes, preserves Google Limited Use boundaries, describes provider routing without blanket no-training/30-day promises, and provides request/appeal routes. Terms preserve the three billing products and existing performance settings, explain renewal and separate cancellation/disconnection/deletion, retain merchant-of-record and third-party constraints, disclose Apply’s publishing behavior, and reserve mandatory legal rights. No arbitration or class-action waiver was added. The default liability floor is now stated in US dollars (US$100) for the Delaware operator.

# Design notes for Figma

## Type scale (use exactly these — keeps it readable when printed)

- Hero stat: **60pt bold**
- Section headlines: **28pt semibold**
- Body copy: **11pt regular**
- Small caps section labels: **10pt tracked +200**
- Footer/source lines: **8pt grey 60%**

## Color (use existing écentic palette)

- Primary blue: as per brand
- Background: white (one-pagers print, dark backgrounds eat ink)
- Accent: single bright blue for the hero stat, the bold timeline line, and the QR
- Text: near-black for body, grey 60% for source lines

## White space rules

- Margins: minimum 15mm on all sides
- Between bands: minimum 8mm vertical
- Never fill more than 80% of the page — leave breathing room

## What to ABSOLUTELY avoid

- Walls of body text (more than 3 lines in any block kills scannability)
- More than 5 logos in the proof strip
- More than 2 type weights (regular + bold/semibold is enough)
- Stock photos of "diverse team in office" — use real founder shots only
- Footer disclaimer paragraphs ("legal", "©", etc.) — kills the energy

## Recommended export

- **PDF (print-quality, 300 DPI)** — for printing handouts
- **PNG (1500px wide)** — for emailing as an inline preview
- **Optional: a "digital" variant** with darker background + QR-only contact, smaller hero stat — if you want to use it as a slide too

---

# Quick checklist before you print

- [ ] All copy fits on ONE page (no overflow)
- [ ] QR code actually scans to your demo URL (test on three phones)
- [ ] Logo files at @2x resolution minimum
- [ ] Photos of founders are recent and consistent in lighting/background
- [ ] No typos in "Plug and Play Tirana" or "Red Bull Basement"
- [ ] Print test on a real A4/Letter sheet — colours always look different on paper
- [ ] Hand to one non-tech friend, ask them to explain écentic in 30 seconds after reading. If they can't, copy needs more work.
